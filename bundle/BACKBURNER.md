# Back burner — parked ideas (not building yet)

## Base44 + blog stack (parked, with a verdict)

**Idea:** use the Base44 integration to also spin up a **blog** — turn the same
content the system produces into SEO blog posts hosted on Base44.

**Verdict: worthwhile, but as a *separate* stack — not part of the core system.**

Why separate, not bolted on:
- The core product's whole promise right now is *"dead simple, one SOP."* Adding a
  blog/SEO/CMS layer bloats that and confuses the buyer.
- A blog is a different job (long-form + SEO + hosting) with a different buyer
  intent than short-form social automation.
- It's a natural *adjacent* product / upsell: **"Blog Engine"** — repurpose your
  posts into SEO articles + host them on Base44, feeding the same funnel
  (social → blog → email). Sells to the same audience, later.

**When to build it:** after the core system is live and selling. Then it becomes
the next rung, not a feature.

Status: **parked.** Do not build. Revisit once the core is shipping.
