# Your ManyChat sales funnel (this is YOURS, not part of the product)

ManyChat is the tool **you** use to sell the bundle. It is not a feature inside
Reel Engine and it is not something a buyer touches. Its only job: when someone
comments a keyword on your post, ManyChat **auto-DMs them the buy link.**

## The flow
```
You post an IG Reel  →  caption/video says "comment LINK"
        │
        ▼
Viewer comments "LINK"  →  ManyChat detects the keyword
        │
        ▼
ManyChat auto-replies to the comment ("sent you a DM! 💌")
        │
        ▼
ManyChat DMs them  →  "Here's the link 👉 [your checkout URL]"
        │
        ▼
They buy the $37 Blueprint (or the bundle)
```

Why this beats "link in bio": it turns a comment into a 1:1 DM with the exact
link, it juices the comment count (reach), and it's the compliant way to move
people off-platform.

## Setup (one time, ~20 min, in ManyChat — not here)
1. Connect your **Instagram** account to ManyChat.
2. Create a **Comment Automation** (a.k.a. "Instagram Comments" trigger).
3. Trigger keyword: **LINK** (also add: `link`, `LINKS`, `ME`, `WELLNESS`).
4. Scope it to **a specific post** (or "any post") — start with per-post.
5. Public comment auto-reply: *"Just sent it to your DMs! 💌 check your messages 👀"*
6. DM message: *"Heyy! Here's the step-by-step system I promised 👉 [CHECKOUT URL]
   — comment if you have any Q's! (affiliate/ad)"*
7. Put your **checkout URL** in the DM (Stripe Payment Link / Gumroad for the $37
   Blueprint, or the bundle page).

## The keyword → offer map (so one account sells the whole ladder)
| Keyword | DM sends them to |
|---------|------------------|
| `LINK` / `BLUEPRINT` | $37 Reel Engine Blueprint checkout |
| `STACK` | $97 Stock Stack checkout |
| `BUNDLE` | $127 bundle checkout |
| `APP` | Reel Engine SaaS waitlist / signup |

## Copy rules (match the honest voice)
- Always include a disclosure in the DM (`affiliate/ad`) — same rule as your posts.
- One link per DM. Don't dump the whole ladder on them; send the one they asked for.
- Keep the public reply short and human; save the pitch for the DM.
