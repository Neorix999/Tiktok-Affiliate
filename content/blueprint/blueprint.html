What this is (read first)

A repeatable system that runs your content like a small team: it researches what to post, creates the visual, writes the caption, schedules it, and posts it — while you approve at four points so nothing goes out you didn't sign off on. It's not tied to a niche. Set it up once (Part 1), then run any account through it (Part 3 shows real examples: Pinterest affiliate, a quote page, a travel account, client work).

The 4 gates you control: ① approve what to post · ② approve the visual · ③ approve the caption + link · ④ final go before it publishes.

Part 1 — Set it up once (A → G)

A. Connect Postiz (your scheduler for Instagram, Pinterest, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, etc.). Sign up here: postiz.pro/melannie-carranza-iglesias. Connect each account you post to.
B. Connect Higgsfield — generates and animates visuals, and publishes directly to TikTok (Postiz can't post TikTok — this is the TikTok rail). [add your Higgsfield affiliate link here]
C. Connect Canva — for graphics, text-on-image (quotes), Pinterest pins, covers, and overlays. [add your Canva affiliate link here]
D. Make your Asset Folder. On your own computer, create one folder for the images/clips you want to use — organized in subfolders (e.g. /products, /travel-photos, /client-name). When you run Claude on your computer (with file access), it reads that folder and pulls the right assets into the content. This is how your real photos get used.
E. Set up your Tracker (Airtable). One row per post, moving through the statuses. It's your control panel and where you approve. (Template provided with this system.)
F. Fill your Account Profile. The one thing that makes it "yours" — fill these five lines once per account: 
PLATFORM:   __________  (IG / Pinterest / TikTok / client's)
OFFER:      __________  (affiliate link / UGC deliverable / client brand)
VOICE:      __________  (funny / aspirational / clean / expert)
LOOK:       __________  (your locked look: light, palette, style)
ASSETS:     __________  (generate fresh / pull from my folder / both)
G. Set your schedule. Pick your slots (e.g. 1/day) and let Postiz publish on time. Refill weekly (Part 5).

Part 2 — How the content actually gets made

The "Create" step, in plain terms. This is what happens after you approve a thing to post:

Your Asset Folder ──▶ Claude reads what's there │ ├─▶ need a NEW visual / animation? ─▶ HIGGSFIELD (generate or animate → 9:16 / 4:5 / pin) ├─▶ need a GRAPHIC / quote / pin / cover? ─▶ CANVA (text-on-image, layouts) └─▶ have a REAL photo to use? ─▶ pull it from the folder, enhance/reframe │ ▼ finished asset ─▶ caption + link + #ad ─▶ into the Tracker for your approval

You tell Claude what you want ("make a pin for this product", "animate my travel photo", "quote graphic in my style"); it routes to Higgsfield or Canva accordingly and pulls from your folder when you point it there.

Part 5 — The weekly loop

ONE SESSION / WEEK: research → create a batch → caption all at once → approve → schedule the week → it publishes itself → check results → next week's research repeats it

Batch by task (all research, then all creation, then all captions) — far faster than doing one post start-to-finish seven times.

The one rule that keeps you safe

Every post with an affiliate link carries a clear disclosure (#ad or equivalent), near the top — not buried. It's FTC-required in the US and costs you nothing in trust. Never promise guaranteed results or income.
