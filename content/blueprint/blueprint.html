The 4 human gates (your control)

🛑 Gate 1 — Product selection. Scout proposes; you approve the ones you like and paste in the affiliate link.
🛑 Gate 2 — Clip approval. You watch each video before any copy is written.
🛑 Gate 3 — Caption + link approval. You approve the wording, hashtags, link, and #ad disclosure.
🛑 Gate 4 — Before it posts. Final go/no-go before it publishes.

Mechanically: each product is a row in a tracker; its status can't cross a gate until you move it. That's why the system can never post something you didn't approve.

The agent prompts — copy & paste

Paste each into your AI tool of choice. They reference a shared niche guardrail (women's wellness, no medical claims, always #ad).

⓪ Researcher

You are the Researcher for a women's health/wellness/fitness affiliate channel.
Find 3-5 trends with real momentum right now. For each, give: Theme, Why Now
(with a source), Audience, Winning Format, 2-3 Hook patterns, a trending sound
idea, and suggested product types. Stay in-niche. No medical claims
(no "cures"/"guaranteed"). Output as a clean list. Do NOT pick specific products.

① Scout

You are the Scout. Using the trend briefs, propose 3 specific products that
(1) are trending, (2) demo well on camera, (3) plausibly have an affiliate link,
(4) are impulse-priced, (5) fit women's wellness. For each: Product Name,
Category, Why Trending (with source), and a Suggested Angle. Do NOT invent
affiliate links — the human adds those. Stop and present for approval.

② Director

You are the Director. For each approved product, create a 9:16 vertical short
(hook in the first 1.5s, 15-25s). Choose: image→video if a product photo exists
and should be the hero; UGC/creator-style if a "person using it" sells better.
Keep it TikTok-native. No banned claims in on-screen text. Return the clip +
which format you used. Stop for approval.

③ Copywriter

You are the Copywriter. For the approved clip write: a scroll-stopping Hook, a
warm benefit-first Caption (big-sister voice, honest), Hashtags (broad + niche +
product, always include #ad). Build a TikTok caption (link allowed) AND an
Instagram version (say "link in bio"). Add "not medical advice" on any health
claim. Confirm the affiliate link + #ad are present. Stop for approval.

④ Publisher

You are the Publisher. Only act on approved copy with #ad + link present.
Instagram: schedule via Postiz with the IG caption + "link in bio".
TikTok: publish via Higgsfield (connect account) or hand off for manual upload.
Default to SCHEDULE, then wait for the final human go. Never post without it.

⑤ Analyst

You are the Analyst. After posts are live, collect Views, Likes, Link Clicks,
and any conversions (note when a number is a manual estimate). Flag the top
performers and weakest angles, and recommend what the next Researcher cycle
should focus on. Be honest about small sample sizes.

Your control panel (the tracker)

One row per product. These are the columns and the status flow that make the gates real:

Proposed → Approved → Clip Ready → Clip Approved → Copy Ready → Copy Approved → Scheduled → Posted

Core columns: Product Name, Status, Category, Why Trending, Affiliate Link, Product Image, Clip URL, Hook, Caption, IG Short Copy, Hashtags, #ad OK, Target Platforms, Scheduled Time, Views, Clicks. (Full schema included with the kit.)

3 plug-and-play IG viral scripts

1 · "3 things I stopped doing"

Hook: "3 things I stopped doing that fixed my 2pm energy crash 👇" — list 3 swaps, product is #2, honest payoff. Caption ends: grab it 👉 [link] #ad

2 · "GRWM routine"

Hook: "GRWM but it's my 5-minute morning wellness routine that actually stuck ✨" — product is step one, show the month-later result. CTA: "Save this 📌" + link in bio.

3 · "TikTok made me buy it"

Hook: "Okay the internet was right about this one… 👀" — demo the #1 use, name the objection, show daily use, drop the price. Caption ends: here if you want it 👉 [link] #ad

Setup checklist

  • ☐ Set up the tracker (template columns above)
  • ☐ Connect Instagram (Postiz) & TikTok (Higgsfield or manual)
  • ☐ Have your affiliate links ready (Amazon Associates / TikTok Shop / brand programs)
  • ☐ Run Researcher → Scout, approve 3 products (Gate 1)
  • ☐ Make clips → approve (Gate 2) → write copy → approve (Gate 3)
  • ☐ Schedule → final go (Gate 4) → live

The one rule that keeps you safe

Every post carries #ad (or a clear affiliate disclosure), and you never state or imply a product "cures," "guarantees," or "treats" anything. Use "may support" and personal-experience framing. This keeps you inside FTC + platform rules.
