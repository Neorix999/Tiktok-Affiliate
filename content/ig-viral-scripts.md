# 3 IG short-form viral video scripts (women's wellness)

Ready to shoot today. Each is **15–25s, vertical 9:16**, built for Reels/TikTok.
Drop your real product + affiliate link into the `[brackets]`. Every one keeps
`#ad` — non-negotiable.

Format of each: **Hook → beat-by-beat → on-screen text → caption → hashtags →
CTA.** Hooks are written to survive the 1.5-second scroll test.

---

## Video 1 — "3 things I stopped doing" (relatable list)
**Best for:** sleep / stress / magnesium / cortisol-type products.
**Hook (0–2s, talking to camera):** "3 things I stopped doing that fixed my
2pm energy crash 👇"
**Beats:**
- 0–2s: hook, direct to camera.
- 2–6s: "① Stopped my 3rd coffee" — cut to you pushing a mug away.
- 6–11s: "② Started [product] at night" — show the product in hand, quick demo.
- 11–16s: "③ Stopped doomscrolling in bed" — phone face-down.
- 16–20s: "Two weeks in and I actually feel rested." — soft smile, product on nightstand.
**On-screen text:** big bold numbered captions, one per beat.
**Caption:**
> The 2pm crash was running my life 😮‍💨 these 3 swaps changed it — the [product]
> at night is the one I didn't expect. Not medical advice, just what worked for
> me 🤍 grab it 👉 [affiliate link] #ad
**Hashtags:** `#wellnesstok #womenshealth #magnesium #sleeptips #selfcare #ad`
**CTA:** "Comment 'REST' and I'll send the link 💌" (drives comments) + link in bio.

## Video 3 — "TikTok made me buy it" (product reveal / demo)
**Best for:** fitness gear / recovery tool / gadget that demos well on camera.
**Hook (0–2s, product already in frame):** "Okay the internet was right about
this one… 👀"
**Beats:**
- 0–2s: hook, hold the product up.
- 2–7s: "Here's what it actually does" — demo the #1 use, satisfying motion.
- 7–12s: "I was skeptical because [common objection]…" — quick honest beat.
- 12–17s: "…but I've used it every day since." — show it in your real space.
- 17–22s: price/impulse line: "And it's under [$X]." — product to camera.
**On-screen text:** "the internet was right →", "under [$X]" sticker at the end.
**Caption:**
> I did not expect to love this 😅 [product] is genuinely part of my [workout/recovery]
> now — honest review, not sponsored fluff. Here if you want it 👉 [affiliate link] #ad
**Hashtags:** `#tiktokmademebuyit #fittok #recovery #wellnesstok #womenshealth #ad`
**CTA:** "Would you try it? 👇" + link in bio.

## Shooting + posting notes
- **Vertical 9:16**, natural light, hook in the **first 1.5s** or they scroll.
- Use a **trending sound** (Researcher/Higgsfield `tiktok_music_trending` surfaces
  current ones) — huge for reach.
- **Link:** IG doesn't allow clickable links in captions → use **link in bio**
  (Postiz/Linktree) + say "link in bio"; TikTok can take the link directly.
- **Disclosure:** `#ad` stays in every caption, and say "not medical advice" on
  any health-benefit claim.
- **Avoid** banned claims (no "cures", "guaranteed", medical promises) — see
  [`../config/niche.md`](../config/niche.md).
- Post 1/day/account; A/B the hooks — keep what holds retention past 3s.
